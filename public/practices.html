Questions

Fair questions.

Do you work outside Texas?

Yes. We're based in Harlingen, Texas and work with practices across the U.S. Everything runs remotely. The monthly on-site shoot in full service social is for South Texas; everywhere else, your team films and we handle the rest.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. We earn the next month by doing the work this month.

What does EMR management include?

A monthly call to go over your account, plus direct support from our team when you need a change or something isn't working. $249 a month, added to any plan.

Are there setup fees?

Only a $399 build fee if you get the website on its own. Add anything else or pick a bundle and it's waived.

Can I keep an agency I like?

Of course. Pick only the pieces you need. If your social person is great, keep them and let us handle the website, Google and EMR.
