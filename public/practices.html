Questions

Fair questions.

Do you work with practices outside Texas?

Yes. We're based in Harlingen, Texas and work with businesses across the U.S. Everything runs remotely. The monthly on-site shoot in Full Service Social is for South Texas; everywhere else, your team films and we handle the rest.

What does EMR Account Management include?

A monthly call to go over your account, plus direct support from our team when you need a change or something isn't working. It's $249 a month, month-to-month, and you can add it to any plan.

Is there a contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month. We earn the next month by doing the work this month.

Are there setup fees?

No activation fees. The only one-time charge is a $399 build fee on the website on its own, and it's waived when you add anything else or pick a bundle.

Who pays for the ads?

You fund the ad spend and Google or Meta bills you directly. Our fee covers building, running and improving the campaigns.

Can I keep an agency I like?

Of course. Pick only the pieces you need. If your social person is great, keep them and let us handle the website, Google and EMR.
